Goals

In the Premier League, a goal is defined as any score that a team scores during a match. This includes both goals scored and conceded. For a goal to count all of the ball must cross the line. (FPL App)

The top 10 all-time goalscorers in the Premier League are:

  1. Alan Shearer (260 Goals)
  2. Harry Kane (213 Goals)
  3. Wayne Rooney (208 Goals)
  4. Andrew Cole (187 Goals)
  5. Mohamed Salah (185 Goals)
  6. Sergio Aguero (184 Goals)
  7. Frank Lampard (177 Goals)
  8. Thierry Henry (175 Goals)
  9. Robbie Fowler (163 Goals)
  10. Jermain Defoe (162 Goals)

During his club career, Alan Shearer played for teams like Southampton F.C., Blackburn Rovers F.C., and most famously Newcastle United F.C.. He won the Premier League title with Blackburn in the 1994–95 season. Alan Shearer retired in 2006.
