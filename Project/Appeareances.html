Appeareances

In the Premier League, an appearance is counted as long as a player is on the field during official league play. This includes matches where the player is in the starting eleven or substitutes. (FPL App)

The top 10 all-time Premier League appearance leaders are:

  1. James Milner (657 Appeareances)
  2. Gareth Barry (653 Appeareances)
  3. Ryan Giggs (632 Appeareances)
  4. Frank Lampard (609 Appeareances)
  5. David James (572 Appeareances)
  6. Gary Speed (535 Appeareances)
  7. Emile Heskey (516 Appeareances)
  8. Mark Schwarzer (514 Appeareances)
  9. Jamie Carragher (508 Appeareances)
  10. Phil Neville (505 Appeareances)

Milner has played for several major clubs, including Leeds United, Aston Villa, Manchester City, Liverpool, and later Brighton & Hove Albion. Throughout his career, he has won major trophies, including the Premier League, FA Cups.
